# Level 1 work

Your Level 1 work goes in this folder. Module 2.2 tells you when to add each piece.

Before your first commit, copy in:

- Your Module 1.1 scenario answers, with your list of things you always ask about before
  pasting
- Your Module 1.2 table of the four building blocks
- Your Module 1.3 PDF, the Cedar Ridge reconciliation
- Your Module 1.4 PDF, the process map

Later in Module 2.2, on a branch, copy in:

- Your Module 1.5 work: your `SKILL.md` file, or the folder that holds it, plus the PDF
  or zip with your packaged instruction and the record of running it twice

That second addition becomes your first pull request.
